06 / Helpful by nature

Writing & interaction

Say what happens next

“Download sample PDF” tells people exactly what they’ll get. “Enter a complete email address” explains how to recover. Keep instructions short and close to the action.

Make the boundaries visible

Separate AI suggestions from document requirements. Explain unsupported formats. Never imply that downloading submits an application or that a prototype processes a real document.
